# Landing Prototype Contract V1

Landing prototype artifacts are draft-only JSON documents. They represent sections and claims for a future start page, but they are not deployed, hosted, published, or connected to any real form.

Required fields:

- artifact id;
- artifact type `landing_prototype`;
- draft-only flag;
- deployment and publish flags fixed to false;
- owner approval requirement for any future deploy or send;
- evidence-backed claims.

Artifacts are hashed with SHA-256 before handoff.
