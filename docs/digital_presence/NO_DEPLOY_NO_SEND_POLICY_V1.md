# No Deploy No Send Policy V1

This stage is no-deploy and no-send.

Forbidden:

- production website deployment;
- publication;
- hosting changes;
- DNS changes;
- VPS changes;
- real browser automation;
- real scraping or crawling;
- form submit;
- outbound email;
- social send;
- Telegram, WhatsApp, VK, MAX, Avito send;
- CRM write;
- production DB write;
- payment or invoice action.

Owner approval is required before any future deployment or send.
