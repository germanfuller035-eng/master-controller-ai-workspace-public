Future Android approval surfaces must show the same fields as the owner approval contract: action, target, reason, amount or scope, risk, payload hash, expiry, changed data, and required owner action.

R5 alignment requires future PIN or biometric confirmation, repeated description, delay, and one-time approval. STOP must display revoked approvals, disabled queues, checkpoint location, and next safe action.
