# Today And Decisions V1

Today answers the morning question: what needs owner attention first?

The panel must show exactly one main action, supporting evidence, urgent decisions, STOP visibility, budget status, and no-send state.

Decisions are ordered with urgent approvals first, then incidents, cost risks, and review-only proposals. A decision cannot claim PASS without evidence.

No decision in this session performs an external action. Approvals are synthetic cards only.
