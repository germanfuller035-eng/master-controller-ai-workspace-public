# Mini-Audit / Digital Presence Draft Template

Use this template to turn manually observed facts into a short draft. Keep real entries outside the repo.

```text
LEAD_ID=
OBSERVED_PUBLIC_PRESENCE=
WEBSITE_OR_PROFILE_NOTES=
VISIBLE_OFFER_OR_POSITIONING=
VISIBLE_FRICTION_POINTS=
MESSAGING_GAPS=
LOCAL_OR_NICHE_CONTEXT=
OWNER_ASSUMPTIONS=
CONFIDENCE=LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH
WHAT_MUST_BE_VERIFIED_BEFORE_SEND=
```

## Guardrails

- Use only facts the owner can verify manually.
- Do not claim private knowledge.
- Do not infer revenue, team size, budget, urgency, or decision-maker identity unless directly verified.
- Do not include scraped personal data or sensitive categories.
