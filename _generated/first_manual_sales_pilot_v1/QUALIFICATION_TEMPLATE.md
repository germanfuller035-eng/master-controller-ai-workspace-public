# Qualification Template

Use this as an owner review sheet. Keep real entries outside the repo.

```text
LEAD_ID=
NICHE_FIT=YES/NO/UNKNOWN
CITY_OR_REGION_FIT=YES/NO/UNKNOWN
PROBLEM_CLARITY=LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH
BUYER_RELEVANCE=LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH
DIGITAL_PRESENCE_VISIBLE=YES/NO/UNKNOWN
CONTACT_CHANNEL_AVAILABLE=YES/NO/UNKNOWN
OWNER_CAN_EXPLAIN_VALUE=YES/NO
DATA_SENSITIVITY=LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH
READY_FOR_DRAFT=YES/NO
MISSING_INFORMATION=
DISQUALIFY_REASON=
OWNER_DECISION=PROCEED/HOLD/DROP
```

## Minimum Gate

Proceed only when:

- The lead is a plausible fit for the offer.
- The problem hints are concrete enough for a useful draft.
- The contact channel is manually verified by the owner.
- The draft does not depend on unsupported claims or private data.
