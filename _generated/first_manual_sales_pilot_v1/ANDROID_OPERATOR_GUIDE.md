# Android Operator Guide

Use this guide for each lead during the manual pilot.

## Per-Lead Steps

1. Open Android Master Controller.
2. Open Today/Home.
3. Open Pipeline or Commercial.
4. Open Working Sales MVP.
5. Add or load the manual/demo lead.
6. Review qualification score, readiness, missing data, and reasons.
7. Review mini-audit / digital presence draft.
8. Review product strategy and product fit reason.
9. Open Offer Preview.
10. Review expected value, scope, risks, non-promises, and next step.
11. Review ROI assumptions.
12. Run the QA/red-team checklist manually.
13. Confirm visible safety state:
    - no-send PASS
    - no-payment PASS
    - no-production-write PASS
14. Use the approval packet as owner decision support only.
15. If owner approves, copy/send manually outside the app.
16. Record outcome manually outside the repo.

## Do Not Use The App For

- Automatic outbound.
- Payment request.
- Production DB write.
- CRM sync.
- Mail, Telegram, social, browser automation, or controlled-send gate.
