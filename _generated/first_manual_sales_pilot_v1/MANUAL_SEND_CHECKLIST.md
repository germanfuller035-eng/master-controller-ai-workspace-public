# Manual Send Checklist

This checklist is for owner action outside the app. The system must not send.

```text
LEAD_ID=
OWNER_APPROVED_MANUAL_SEND=YES/NO
CHANNEL=
MESSAGE_COPIED_OUTSIDE_APP=YES/NO
SENT_BY_SYSTEM=NO
SENT_MANUALLY=YES/NO
DATE=
NOTES=
```

## Required Before Manual Send

- The owner selected the lead manually.
- The owner reviewed qualification.
- The owner reviewed mini-audit and product strategy.
- The owner reviewed offer draft and ROI assumptions.
- QA/red-team checklist passed.
- No-send / no-payment / no-production-write status is PASS.
- No CRM, payment, mail, Telegram, social, browser automation, or production DB connector is used.

## Manual Send Rule

The owner may copy text and send it manually outside the app only after explicit owner decision.
