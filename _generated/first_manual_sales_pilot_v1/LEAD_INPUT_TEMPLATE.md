Do not commit real lead data to git.

```text

NICHE=

CONTACT_CHANNEL=
CONTACT_PERSON_OPTIONAL=
PROBLEM_HINTS=

OWNER_NOTES=
DATA_SENSITIVITY=LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH
PERMISSION_TO_USE_IN_SYSTEM=YES/NO
```

## Synthetic Dry-Run Rule

Committed examples must remain synthetic only. Do not include real company names, real websites, real domains, real people, emails, phone numbers, social handles, CRM ids, or private notes.

## Owner Review Before Use

- The lead was manually selected by the owner.
- The source is lawful and appropriate for manual outreach.
- No sensitive personal, military, medical, financial, or protected data is included.
- Permission field is explicitly set before entering anything into the app.
