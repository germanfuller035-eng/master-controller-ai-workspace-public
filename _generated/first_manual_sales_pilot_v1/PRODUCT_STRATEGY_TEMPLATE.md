# Product Strategy Template

Use this to decide which product angle fits the lead. Keep real entries outside the repo.

```text
LEAD_ID=
PRIMARY_PRODUCT=
ALTERNATIVE_PRODUCT=
PRODUCT_FIT_REASON=
CUSTOMER_PROBLEM_MATCH=
WHY_THIS_NOW=
OWNER_CONFIDENCE=LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH
WHAT_TO_AVOID=
POSITIONING_NOTES=
NEXT_REVIEW_STEP=
```

## Strategy Gate

The product angle is ready only when the owner can explain:

- The observable problem.
- Why the product is relevant.
- What value is expected.
- What is not promised.
- What the next step asks the lead to do.
