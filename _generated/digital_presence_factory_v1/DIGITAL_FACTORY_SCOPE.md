# Digital Factory Scope

IN_SCOPE:

- Digital Presence Check contract;
- website analysis contract;
- landing/start-page/website prototype draft contract;
- Lead System architecture handoff;
- AI Front Office architecture handoff;
- local deterministic no-deploy shadow pipeline;
- synthetic fixtures;
- QA / Red Team;
- artifact hashing;
- evidence/checkpoint/handoff.

OUT_OF_SCOPE:

- production website deployment;
- DNS or VPS changes;
- real browser automation;
- real website scraping/crawling;
- external form submit;
- outbound email/social/messenger send;
- CRM write;
- production DB write;
- payment or invoice actions;
- real client data ingestion.
