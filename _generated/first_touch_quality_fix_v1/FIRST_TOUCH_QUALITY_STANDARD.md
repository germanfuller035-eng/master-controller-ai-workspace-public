# First-Touch Quality Standard

STATUS=ACTIVE_FOR_FIRST_MANUAL_SALES_PILOT

## Required Shape

Every first-touch draft must include:

- human intro: Дмитрий introduces himself;
- work context: site and first-touch review for B2B / production-style companies;
- visible context: only what can be seen from public company/page/site context;
- broad hypothesis: what a new customer usually needs to understand;
- certainty caveat: no claim that the lead definitely has a problem;
- concrete small offer: external mini-review on 3-5 points;
- low-pressure CTA: ask who should receive the review.

## Forbidden In First Touch

- ROI, traffic, conversion, growth, or client-volume claims;
- exact-problem language such as "we found your problem";
- pricing, payment, invoice, contract, or launch pressure;
- abstract internal phrases such as public digital presence or first-response friction;
- unsupported claims, fake certainty, screenshots, private evidence, or attachments.
